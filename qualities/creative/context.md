## What It Means

Creativity is the capacity to generate novel ideas, connections, and solutions — to see what isn't there yet and bring it into being. It's not limited to art; it's a mode of thinking and living available to everyone.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Creative thinking is problem-solving at its best. It's also one of the deepest sources of joy available to human beings. Making something new — in any domain — is an act of aliveness.

---

## Action Steps

- Make something today that didn't exist this morning — write, draw, cook, build, compose
- When facing a problem today, force yourself to come up with 5 different solutions before picking one
- Expose yourself to something completely outside your normal inputs — art, music, a different neighborhood
