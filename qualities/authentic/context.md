# Authentic

*Page 7 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

---

## What It Means

Authenticity is the practice of aligning your outer expression with your inner truth — values, feelings, and self — without distorting yourself to meet the expectations of others. It requires courage, self-knowledge, and a willingness to be seen.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

The exhaustion of performing a version of yourself is real. The freedom of not needing to is one of the most transformative experiences available to a human being. Authenticity isn't a luxury — it's a foundation.

---

## Action Steps

- Notice one place today where you're performing instead of being — and gently stop
- Share one real opinion or feeling with someone close to you this week
- Ask yourself: what would I do differently if I weren't trying to manage anyone's perception of me?
