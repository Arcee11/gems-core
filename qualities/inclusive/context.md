*Page 23 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

## What It Means

Inclusion is the active practice of ensuring people feel welcome, heard, valued, and belonging — not as a policy, but as a human commitment. It's noticing who's missing, who's quiet, and whose voice hasn't been heard.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Diverse perspectives produce better decisions. Environments where people feel included produce more creativity, more engagement, and better outcomes. Inclusion isn't charity — it's excellence.

---

## Action Steps

- In your next group setting, notice who is quiet. Create space for that person.
- Seek out a perspective today that is different from your own — and genuinely engage with it
- When making a decision that affects others, ask: who have I not heard from yet?
