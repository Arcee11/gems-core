# Peaceful
*Page 29 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

> **Finding and maintaining inner calm, even in the presence of chaos or difficulty.**

---

## How to Embody This Word

To embody peace is not to eliminate conflict or difficulty — it is to find a steady, calm center that holds even when the world around you is turbulent. Peace is an inner state you cultivate, not a circumstance you wait for. It comes from acceptance, from slowing down to breathe, from releasing the need to control what is outside your influence.

---

## Where to Start & How to Get There

- **Create a Peace Ritual:** Choose one simple practice — deep breathing, a short walk, five minutes of stillness — and do it every morning before your day begins. This anchors peace into your nervous system before the noise arrives.
- **Practice Acceptance:** Identify one thing in your life right now that you cannot change. Write it down and then write the words: 'I release my grip on this.' Practice letting it be what it is.
- **Set One Boundary Against Chaos:** Identify one source of unnecessary stress in your daily life — a habit, a situation, or an input — and make one change to reduce its influence.
