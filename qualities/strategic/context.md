## What It Means

Strategic thinking is the capacity to step back from immediate pressures, see the larger pattern, and make decisions that serve long-term goals rather than short-term comfort. It's the discipline of asking 'where is this going?' before deciding 'what do I do now?'

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Busyness without strategy is just motion. Strategic thinkers do less and achieve more — because they direct their effort toward the moves that matter most. In a world of infinite options, the ability to choose wisely is everything.

---

## Action Steps

- Before your most important work today, take 10 minutes to ask: what's the highest-leverage thing I could do right now?
- Map out the next 30 days for one important goal — identify the three moves that will matter most
- Identify one thing you're doing that doesn't serve your long-term goals — and question whether to stop
