## What It Means

Cooperation is the practice of working in willing coordination with others toward shared goals — contributing your part, honoring agreements, and prioritizing collective outcomes over individual wins.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Most meaningful things in life require cooperation. Relationships, families, organizations, and communities all run on the willingness of individuals to coordinate. Cooperation isn't giving up — it's scaling up.

---

## Action Steps

- In your next group context, do your part thoroughly — without being asked twice
- Look for one place where a small cooperation on your part would make a big difference for someone else
- Practice saying 'what can I do to help move this forward?'
