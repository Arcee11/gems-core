## What It Means

To inspire is to elevate another person's sense of what's possible — for themselves, their situation, or the world. It comes not from performance but from genuine aliveness: the inspiration of someone who is actually living their values.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Inspiration is contagious. One person living with conviction can change the trajectory of everyone in their orbit. You don't have to be famous to be inspiring — you have to be real.

---

## Action Steps

- Share something you're genuinely excited about with someone today — let your real enthusiasm show
- Tell someone what you believe they're capable of — specifically and sincerely
- Do something today that reflects your values fully — not for any audience, just because it's true
