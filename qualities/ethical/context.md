## What It Means

Ethics is the practice of making choices aligned with clearly held moral principles — honesty, fairness, care for others — even when it's costly, inconvenient, or unnoticed. It's integrity as a way of life, not a pose.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Your reputation is built in the moments no one sees. Character is what you do when there are no witnesses and no consequences — and it compounds over time into a life you're proud of or a life you can't quite look at.

---

## Action Steps

- Notice one small ethical moment today — and make the right call, even if no one would know
- Identify any area of your life where you're rationalizing something you know isn't right — and address it
- Hold one standard higher than required today — in your work, your relationships, or your commitments
