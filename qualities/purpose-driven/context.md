# Purpose Driven
*Page 33 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

> **Living and acting in alignment with a meaningful mission that is bigger than yourself.**

---

## How to Embody This Word

To be purpose-driven is to anchor every decision, effort, and relationship to something that matters deeply — a cause, a vision, or a calling that extends beyond personal comfort or achievement. It means asking not just 'What do I want?' but 'What am I here to contribute?' Purpose gives your daily actions weight and direction, transforming ordinary tasks into steps on a meaningful mission.

---

## Where to Start & How to Get There

- **Name Your Why:** Write a single sentence that captures what you are here to contribute or create. This is your personal mission statement. It doesn't have to be perfect — it just has to be true.
- **Align Today with Your Why:** Look at your schedule for today. Identify one activity that directly serves your purpose. Do it first, with full intention.
- **Let Purpose Guide One Decision:** Today, when you face any choice — how to spend time, who to talk to, what to say yes to — run it through your purpose filter: Does this move me toward my mission?
