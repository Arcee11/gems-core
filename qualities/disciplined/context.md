## What It Means

Discipline is the capacity to do what needs to be done regardless of mood, motivation, or momentary resistance. It's not rigidity — it's the reliable structure that makes everything else possible.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Motivation is unreliable. Discipline is not. The people who consistently produce great work, maintain great health, and build great relationships do so not because they always feel like it — but because they've built systems that don't require feeling like it.

---

## Action Steps

- Do your most important task first today — before anything easier or more comfortable
- Identify one habit you're trying to build and remove the friction that makes it hard
- Keep one commitment you made to yourself this week, even when it would be easy to skip
