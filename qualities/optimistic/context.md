## What It Means

Optimism is not naivety — it's a trained way of interpreting events that emphasizes what's possible, what can be learned, and what might go right — while remaining clear-eyed about what's actually true.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Optimists don't just feel better — they perform better. Research consistently shows that optimistic people have better health outcomes, more persistent effort, and higher achievement. Optimism is learnable and it changes outcomes.

---

## Action Steps

- For one current challenge, write out the best realistic outcome — then take one action toward it
- When something goes wrong today, ask: what's still possible from here?
- Notice and challenge one pessimistic thought today — is it actually true, or just familiar?
