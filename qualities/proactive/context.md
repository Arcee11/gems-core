# Proactive

*Page 31 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

---

## What It Means

Proactivity is taking initiative — anticipating what's needed, taking action before circumstances demand it, and choosing your response rather than waiting to react. It's the difference between driving your life and being driven by it.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Reactive living is exhausting — always catching up, always responding to emergencies. Proactive living gives you agency, prevents problems before they start, and puts you in the position of author rather than character.

---

## Action Steps

- Look at the next two weeks and identify one thing that will become urgent if you don't address it now — address it today
- Make one decision today that's forward-looking — for your future self, not just your present comfort
- Start one conversation or task today that you've been waiting for someone else to start
