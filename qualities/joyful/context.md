## What It Means

Joy is a deeper, more durable state than happiness — it's the sense of aliveness and appreciation that persists even through difficulty. And unlike happiness, joy can be cultivated deliberately through attention, practice, and the choice to notice what's good.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Joy is not a reward for when everything goes right. It's a practice that changes how you experience everything. People who cultivate joy are more resilient, more creative, more connected — and more fun to be around.

---

## Action Steps

- Do one thing today purely because it brings you joy — with no productivity attached
- Write down three moments from the last week that contained genuine joy — even small ones
- Bring one playful element into something ordinary today
