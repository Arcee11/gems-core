## What It Means

Resourcefulness is the ability to find creative solutions with the materials, relationships, and capabilities available — without waiting for ideal conditions. It's the mindset that says 'I can work with this' rather than 'I can't do this without X.'

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Nobody ever has all the resources they want. The people who build things, solve problems, and make progress are the ones who start with what they have. Resourcefulness turns constraints into catalysts.

---

## Action Steps

- Identify one thing you've been waiting on external resources for — and find a way to make progress without them
- Look at a current problem and list every asset you already have that could help solve it
- Ask: who do I already know who has dealt with this? Then ask them.
