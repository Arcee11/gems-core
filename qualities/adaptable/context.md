# Adaptable

*Page 2 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

## What It Means

Adaptability is the capacity to adjust your thinking, behavior, and plans in response to new information, changing circumstances, or unexpected obstacles — without losing your core. It's not indecision. It's intelligent flexibility.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

The world rewards those who can pivot. Rigid plans meet rigid endings. The people who flourish long-term are rarely the ones with the best original plan — they're the ones who updated fastest when conditions changed.

---

## Action Steps

- When your plan gets disrupted today, pause before reacting — ask: what's possible now?
- Practice saying 'I was wrong' or 'I need to adjust' once this week
- Try one thing a completely different way than you normally would
