## What It Means

Trustworthiness is built through the consistent alignment of your words and actions over time — doing what you say, being honest when it's hard, and showing up reliably enough that people genuinely believe you will.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Trust is the currency of every meaningful relationship and every functioning organization. It takes a long time to build and a moment to destroy. Being genuinely trustworthy — not just trustworthy when it's convenient — is one of the highest qualities a person can develop.

---

## Action Steps

- Honor every commitment you made this week — however small
- If you can't keep a commitment, tell the person before the deadline, not after
- Be honest in one conversation today where dishonesty or softening would have been easier
