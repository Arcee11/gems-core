## What It Means

Mindfulness is the practice of bringing full, non-judgmental attention to the present moment — what you're doing, feeling, thinking, sensing right now. It's the antidote to the scattered, reactive, half-present state that has become the default of modern life.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

The mind spends over 40% of its time not paying attention to what it's actually doing. Mindfulness reclaims that attention — and with it, the quality of your experience, your decisions, and your relationships.

---

## Action Steps

- Spend five minutes in complete silence today — just observing whatever arises
- Do one activity today with complete, undivided attention — no multitasking
- When your mind wanders today, notice it without judgment and gently return it
