## What It Means

Accountability is the practice of taking ownership over your actions, decisions, and their outcomes — without blame-shifting, excuse-making, or disappearing when things get hard. It's the quality that makes you someone others can rely on, and someone you can rely on yourself.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Without accountability, growth stalls. You can have brilliant insights, strong intentions, and genuine desire to change — but without the follow-through that accountability demands, none of it becomes real.

---

## Action Steps

- Make a commitment today and track it — write it down and check in tomorrow
- When something goes wrong, ask: what was my role in this? — before looking outward
- Tell one person what you're working on this week. Let them ask you about it.
