## What It Means

Influence is the capacity to shape the thinking, feelings, and actions of others — not through authority or manipulation, but through trust, clarity, and genuine care for their wellbeing. Real influence leads people toward their best outcomes.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Influence is unavoidable — you're always affecting the people around you. The question is whether you're doing it intentionally and in a direction that serves them. Developing your influence means developing your ability to lead, parent, teach, and inspire.

---

## Action Steps

- Share an idea or perspective today that you believe in — make the case for it clearly and compellingly
- Identify one person in your life you have influence over. Use it intentionally toward their good today.
- Practice telling a story instead of making an argument — stories move people, data rarely does
