# Balanced

*Page 8 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

---

## What It Means

Balance isn't the absence of intensity — it's the ability to move between intensity and rest, engagement and withdrawal, effort and ease in ways that sustain you over time. A balanced life isn't flat; it's rhythmic.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Burnout is unbalance taken too far in one direction. Stagnation is unbalance taken too far in another. Balance is the practice that keeps you in the game long enough to actually win it.

---

## Action Steps

- Map your current week: where are you over-indexed? Under-indexed?
- Protect one thing this week that restores you — and don't negotiate it away
- Identify one area where you're giving too much and one where you're giving too little
