## What It Means

Charisma is not a personality type — it's a skill. At its core, it's the ability to make the people you're with feel genuinely important, heard, and valued. It's warmth and presence, not performance.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Charisma opens doors that talent alone can't. More importantly, it's the quality that makes your relationships, leadership, and communication land — not just intellectually, but emotionally.

---

## Action Steps

- Give someone your full, undivided attention in your next conversation — no phone, no half-listening
- Practice remembering and using people's names
- Ask one genuine, curious question per conversation today — and actually listen to the answer
