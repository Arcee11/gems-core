## What It Means

Generosity is the practice of giving — time, attention, resources, credit, care — beyond what is expected or required. It's an orientation toward abundance rather than scarcity, and toward others rather than only toward self.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Generosity creates the conditions for deep relationships, trusted reputations, and communities that function. It also changes the giver — people who practice generosity consistently report higher wellbeing, more meaning, and less anxiety about scarcity.

---

## Action Steps

- Give something today without expecting anything in return — time, help, money, credit
- Look for a small way to exceed someone's expectations today
- Share something valuable — knowledge, a connection, an opportunity — with someone who needs it
