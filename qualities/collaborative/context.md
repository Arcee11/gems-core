*Page 10 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

## What It Means

Collaboration is the capacity to work alongside others in ways that combine strengths, honor differences, and produce results that none of you could reach alone. It requires humility, communication, and genuine investment in shared outcomes.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

The world's most important achievements are collaborative. No great company, movement, or idea reached its potential through one person. Collaboration isn't about liking everyone — it's about building something worth building.

---

## Action Steps

- In your next group project or meeting, focus on elevating someone else's idea rather than advancing your own
- Ask a colleague what they need from you this week
- Credit one person publicly this week for a contribution that often goes unnoticed
