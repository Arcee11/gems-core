## What It Means

Innovation is the capacity to see what exists and imagine what could exist instead — then build it. It's creative problem-solving with stakes, applied to real challenges and directed toward real improvement.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

The world doesn't need more people who do things the way they've always been done. It needs people who look at a broken system, a stubborn problem, or an unmet need and say: I can do this differently.

---

## Action Steps

- Identify one thing in your life or work that frustrates you repeatedly. Spend 20 minutes genuinely trying to solve it differently.
- Ask 'what if we did the opposite?' about one assumption in your work or life
- Expose yourself to an industry, field, or discipline completely outside your own — look for transferable ideas
