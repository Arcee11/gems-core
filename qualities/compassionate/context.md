# Compassionate

*Page 11 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

---

## What It Means

Compassion is empathy in motion — not just the ability to feel what another person is experiencing, but the willingness to do something about it. It's care that shows up, not care that stays comfortable.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Compassion is the quality that makes relationships real and communities livable. It also makes you more effective — people who feel cared for perform better, open up more, and stay longer.

---

## Action Steps

- Notice when someone near you is struggling today — and do something, even small
- Practice compassion toward yourself: speak to yourself the way you'd speak to a friend in the same situation
- Ask someone 'how are you actually doing?' and wait for the real answer
