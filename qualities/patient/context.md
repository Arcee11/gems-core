## What It Means

Patience is the capacity to tolerate delay, difficulty, and uncertainty without being driven by anxiety or urgency into premature action. It's not passivity — it's trust in the process while doing your part consistently.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

We live in an impatient culture that rewards speed and punishes waiting. But the deepest things — relationships, mastery, trust, growth — don't respond to urgency. They respond to time and consistent effort.

---

## Action Steps

- Identify one area where impatience is making you make worse decisions. Practice waiting.
- When you feel the urge to rush today, pause and ask: what would happen if I let this take longer?
- Work on one thing today knowing the result won't come today — and be at peace with that
