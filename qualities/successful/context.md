## What It Means

Success, properly understood, is the consistent progress toward goals that are meaningful to you — not a performance for others, not a destination, but an alignment between what you value and how you live. Real success is self-defined.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Most people are chasing someone else's definition of success. The exhaustion and emptiness that follows winning the wrong game is one of the most common human experiences. Defining success clearly — for yourself — is the prerequisite for everything.

---

## Action Steps

- Write your personal definition of success in 3–5 sentences — not what success looks like to others, but to you
- Evaluate your current life against your own definition: where are you aligned? Where aren't you?
- Remove or reduce one activity that serves someone else's definition of success but not your own
