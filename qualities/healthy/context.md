# Healthy

*Page 22 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

## What It Means

Health is the intentional care of your physical body — sleep, movement, nutrition, and rest — as the foundation upon which every other quality is built. It's not vanity; it's infrastructure.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Everything you want to do — think clearly, feel well, show up for others, build something great — runs on the engine of your physical health. Neglect the body long enough and everything else becomes harder.

---

## Action Steps

- Get 7-8 hours of sleep tonight — treat it as non-negotiable
- Move your body for at least 30 minutes today in a way that feels good
- Eat one meal today with genuine care — choose ingredients that make your body feel good
