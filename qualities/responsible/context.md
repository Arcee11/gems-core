## What It Means

Responsibility is the practice of taking ownership of your role, your commitments, and the impact of your actions on others — without blame-shifting or excuse-making. It's being someone the world can count on.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Trust is built through demonstrated responsibility. Every time you do what you said you'd do, honor your commitments, and own your impact — you're building the currency that makes every relationship, career, and community better.

---

## Action Steps

- Make an explicit commitment today and honor it fully
- Look at one area of your life where you've been blaming circumstances or others — take ownership of your part
- Anticipate one way your actions might affect someone else today and factor it in before you act
