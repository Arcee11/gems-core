## What It Means

Gratitude is the practiced capacity to notice and appreciate what is good, present, and working in your life — including the hard things that have taught you something. It's a discipline of attention, not a toxic positivity.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Gratitude is one of the most researched positive psychology interventions. Regular gratitude practice reduces anxiety, increases life satisfaction, strengthens relationships, and retrains the brain toward noticing what works rather than defaulting to threat.

---

## Action Steps

- Write down five specific things you're grateful for today — not the usual answers, dig deeper
- Express gratitude directly to one person today — specifically and sincerely
- Find the gratitude in one current difficulty — what is it teaching you?
