# Empowered
*Page 4 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

> **Having the knowledge, confidence or authority to make decisions and take control of your life.**

---

## How to Embody This Word

To embody empowerment is to claim ownership of your choices and direction rather than waiting for circumstances or others to dictate your path. It means recognizing your inherent ability to influence outcomes, even when situations feel uncertain or overwhelming. Empowered individuals make intentional decisions and seek knowledge or resources to strengthen their agency.

---

## Where to Start & How to Get There

- **Clarify Your Values:** Decisions are easier when aligned with clear priorities. Write down what matters most to you — your top three to five values. When you know what you stand for, every choice becomes more natural.
- **Take One Ownership Step:** Choose one area of your life where you'll make a deliberate choice today instead of defaulting to circumstance. Even one intentional decision builds the empowerment muscle.
- **Learn & Equip Yourself:** Identify a skill or knowledge gap that limits you and take the first step to fill it. Empowerment comes from knowing you have what it takes — or knowing how to get it.
