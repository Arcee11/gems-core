# Confident
*Page 3 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

> **Having a feeling or belief that you can do something well or succeed at something.**

---

## How to Embody This Word

Confidence is a steady belief in your ability to learn, grow, and handle challenges. To embody confidence means trusting your preparation, showing up even when you're nervous, and embracing feedback as part of progress. It's a quiet strength that allows you to act decisively without arrogance, knowing your worth while respecting others.

---

## Where to Start & How to Get There

- **Acknowledge Competence:** Write down three skills or strengths you've demonstrated — things you've done well or improved at. Seeing your competence on paper builds the neural pathways of self-belief.
- **Set a Small Stretch Goal:** Choose a manageable challenge that's just outside your comfort zone and take one concrete action today. Each small stretch widens the territory of what feels possible.
- **Practice Positive Posture & Speech:** Stand tall, make eye contact, and use affirming language. Your body and words shape your inner state — acting confident gradually makes you feel confident.
