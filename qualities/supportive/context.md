# Supportive

*Page 37 of 40 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

---

## What It Means

Supportiveness is the practice of showing up for others — not just in theory but in action — in ways that are actually helpful: listening, following through, being present, and adapting to what the person actually needs rather than what's easiest to give.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

The people who show up when it's hard are the people we build our lives around. Supportiveness isn't passive — it's active presence, offered consistently. It creates trust, deepens bonds, and makes you someone others genuinely want in their lives.

---

## Action Steps

- Ask one person in your life 'what do you need from me right now?' — and actually deliver it
- Follow up with one person who is going through something difficult — not because you have to, because you care
- The next time someone comes to you with a problem, ask 'do you want advice or do you want to be heard?' before offering either
