## What It Means

Focus is the trained capacity to direct your attention fully toward one thing at a time — and to protect that attention from the constant pull of distraction, urgency, and interruption. It is one of the rarest and most valuable abilities in the modern world.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Deep work produces better outcomes than scattered work. Focus is the multiplier on your intelligence and effort. The ability to truly concentrate for extended periods is becoming increasingly rare — and increasingly valuable.

---

## Action Steps

- Schedule one 90-minute block today with zero interruptions — phone off, notifications off, one task
- Before starting any task, write down exactly what done looks like — then pursue only that
- Identify your biggest recurring distraction and remove it for one day
