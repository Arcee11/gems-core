## What It Means

Determination is the sustained commitment to a goal in the face of difficulty, setbacks, and the pull to quit. It's not the absence of doubt — it's the decision to continue in spite of it.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Most worthwhile things take longer than expected and require more than originally planned. Determination is what keeps you in the arena when the initial enthusiasm fades and the real work begins.

---

## Action Steps

- Identify one goal you've been pursuing inconsistently. Recommit to it today — in writing.
- When you hit resistance today, pause before you quit. Ask: is this a signal to stop, or discomfort to move through?
- Remove one obstacle between you and your most important current goal
