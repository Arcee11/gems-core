# Resilient
*Page 2 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

> **To recover strength and momentum after setbacks by using challenges to grow stronger.**

---

## How to Embody This Word

To embody resilience is to accept that setbacks are part of every journey and to rise again without losing your sense of purpose. It's the ability to bend without breaking, to learn from challenges rather than be defined by them. Acknowledge your struggles, adapt and keep moving forward with hope and resolve.

---

## Where to Start & How to Get There

- **Identify a Past Comeback:** Recall a time you overcame difficulty and list three actions or supports that helped. This reminds your brain that you have already proven you can rise.
- **Build Micro Habits for Stability:** Anchor your day with one grounding habit — morning stretching, evening journaling, or a five-minute walk — to create a reliable foundation when everything else feels uncertain.
- **Seek Support Early:** Share your goals or struggles with a trusted person or group. Resilience grows stronger in connection — you are not meant to face hard things alone.
