# Ambitious
*Page 19 — Where to Start and How to Get There*

> **Having a strong desire to achieve great things and the drive to pursue them.**

---

## How to Embody This Word

To be ambitious is to refuse to settle for a life smaller than what you're capable of. It means holding a vision for what could be and working toward it with energy and commitment — even when the path is unclear or the timeline feels long. Healthy ambition is not about ego or comparison; it's about honoring the potential you carry and giving it the space to grow.

---

## Where to Start & How to Get There

- **Define Your Ambitious Vision:** Write a description of the life or achievement you are truly reaching for — not the safe version, the real one. Be specific. Give it color and detail.
- **Break It Into Milestones:** Take your vision and identify three to five major milestones that stand between where you are and where you want to be. Breaking a big dream into visible steps makes it actionable.
- **Schedule Your First Move:** Ambition without action is just daydreaming. Choose the single most important action that moves you toward your vision and put it in your calendar for this week.
