## What It Means

Empathy is the capacity to feel and understand another person's emotional experience — not just intellectually, but from within their perspective. It's the bridge between individuals that makes real connection possible.

---

## Why This Quality Matters

Empathy is the core of every meaningful relationship — personal and professional. It's also a leadership superpower: the leader who understands their people at an emotional level makes better decisions and builds stronger teams.

---

## Action Steps

- In your next conversation with someone struggling, focus entirely on understanding them — not fixing or advising
- Ask 'what was that like for you?' instead of jumping to solutions
- Practice imagining the full context of someone whose behavior frustrates you — what might they be carrying?
